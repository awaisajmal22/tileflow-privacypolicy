Privacy Policy

TileFlow: Color Sort Puzzle · Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

TileFlow ("the App") is developed and published by VConekt LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. The short version: TileFlow does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not show ads. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

DataWhere it is storedPurpose
Game progress (levels completed, coins, unlocked designs, stats)On your device onlyTo save your game between sessions
Display name and avatar you choose in the AppOn your device onlyShown on your in-app profile. Not transmitted to us.
Settings (sound, vibration)On your device onlyTo remember your preferences
Crash report (technical error details, if the App crashes)On your device onlyShown to you on next launch so you can choose to copy and send it to us

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. The App does not use analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs.

2. In-app purchases

TileFlow offers optional in-app purchases (premium bottle designs, backgrounds and coin packs). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never receive your payment details. The store provides the App with an anonymous purchase token used only to unlock the item you bought and to restore purchases on reinstall. Google's and Apple's handling of payment data is governed by their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Internet access

The App requests network access solely so the store billing service can verify and restore purchases. All levels, artwork and sounds are bundled with the App, and it can be played fully offline.

4. Children's privacy

TileFlow is suitable for all ages and is not directed specifically at children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information, no data about children is collected or shared. In-app purchases require the device's store account, which is controlled by the account holder and any parental controls set on the device.

5. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. The only third-party services involved are the app store billing services described in section 2.

6. Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device. You can erase it at any time by using Settings → Reset progress inside the App, or by uninstalling the App. Purchases remain linked to your store account so they can be restored.

7. Security

Because no personal data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side data for us to protect. Local save data is stored in the App's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, these rights are fully satisfied by the on-device controls described above. If you have any question, contact us and we will respond promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App's features change (for example, if we add online features in a future version). Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and material changes will be noted in the App's release notes.
